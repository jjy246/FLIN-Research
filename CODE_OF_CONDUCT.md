# FLIN Community Code of Conduct

This project welcomes contributors of all experience levels and disciplines.
Discuss **ideas, assumptions, code and evidence** rather than attacking people.

- Be respectful. No harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing or personal
  insults.
- Provide reproducible evidence where possible; distinguish hypotheses from
  validated results, and acknowledge uncertainty and limitations.
- Attribute others' code, writings and datasets accurately. Do not share
  private data, credentials or non-redistributable material.
- Respect the project's review process even when your proposal is declined.
- Be constructive when reviewing work by beginners or specialists from another
  field. Rejecting a claim is compatible with treating its author respectfully.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful comments, close issues, reject submissions
or restrict participation following violations. For private conduct reports,
use the repository owner's GitHub profile contact information; **do not post
private reports to public Issues**. For exploitable vulnerabilities use
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
